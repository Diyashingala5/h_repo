01. Create the loan

A structured loan form with the details that matter.

Configure the customer, loan type, amount, rate, installment count and frequency. The system uses the entered rate and frequency to calculate the corresponding period rate.

02. Installment schedule

Know what is due, when it is due, and how it is split.

The installment table provides a line-by-line view of upcoming repayment data, including payment date, pending amount, principal, interest, penalty and received amount.

05. Fees, expenses & accounting

Configure loan journals, interest accounts, penalty accounts, products and sequences. Add processing fees or other charges and keep related invoices and payment entries accessible from the loan.

  • Documents
  • Moves / payment entries

  • Additional expenses
06. Repayment

Make repayment handling simple and traceable.

Clear individual installments, register multiple installments together, receive advance amounts, or process loan cancellation with the applicable calculated amounts.

1 Register payment
2 Receive payment / advance
3 Track remaining balance
4 Close the loan when cleared
Late payments

Configurable penalty handling.

If an installment is paid after its due date, penalty charges can be applied according to the configured company settings. A debit note can be created separately for applicable bank-fee or penalty charges.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I create different loan types?

Yes. Loan types are configurable from the module's Types master data, where the relevant calculation and accounting settings can be maintained.

Can I manage gold or vehicle loans?

Yes. The loan record can show dedicated Gold or Vehicle information depending on the selected loan type.

Can I track overdue installments?

Yes. The Installments workspace supports Today, Upcoming and Overdue filtering, along with custom filters and grouping.

Can I generate loan reports?

Yes. The module provides Loan Statement and Partner Ledger reports and supports analytical Odoo views for loan data.

Can late-payment penalties be handled?

Yes. Penalty charges can be configured and applied when installments are paid after their due date, with separate debit-note handling where configured.
